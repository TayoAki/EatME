2. Information we collect

We collect information you give us, information created when you use the Service, and a limited amount of information from the provider you sign in with.

Account information

EatME has no passwords. You sign in with Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google, which is handled by our authentication provider, Clerk. The provider you choose shares your name, email address and profile photo (if you have one) with us, together with an identifier for your account. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email address, we receive a private relay address instead of your real one. We never receive your Apple or Google password.

Onboarding answers and your plan

To create your plan, we ask for your gender, date of birth, height, current weight, goal (lose, maintain or gain weight), target weight, activity level, weekly pace and diet type (for example, vegetarian), as well as whether you prefer metric or imperial units. We also store your device's time zone so that your days, history and streaks follow your local time.

From these answers we create your plan: daily targets for calories, protein, carbohydrates and fat, a short summary, and a note of whether the plan was generated by AI or by a standard formula. While you complete onboarding, your answers are kept on your device and are saved to your account when you sign up. To show you a plan before you sign up, your answers are also sent to our servers and our AI provider (see Section 4).

Meal photos and your meal log

When you scan a meal, we collect the photo you take or choose from your gallery. For each meal we store the photo, the meal name, the estimated calories, protein, carbohydrates and fat, the time it was logged and any changes you make to those values. We use the days on which you logged meals to show your history and to calculate your streak.

Feedback and messages

If you send feedback from the app, we receive your message and, if you choose to add them, your email address and a screenshot. A screenshot contains whatever is on your screen at that moment, so please check it before sending. If you email us, we receive your email address and the content of your message.

Diagnostic and technical information

To keep EatME reliable, the app uses Sentry, an error monitoring service, to collect:

  • Crash reports and errors: what went wrong and where, with technical details such as your device model, operating system and app version.
  • Diagnostic logs: short records of important events in the app, such as a failed upload or a completed meal analysis. These can include limited details, such as a meal's calorie estimate or your goal type.
  • Performance data: how long screens and network requests take, collected for a sample of sessions.
  • Session replays: for a sample of sessions, and for sessions in which an error occurs, a visual reconstruction of the screens you used and how you interacted with them. In the production app, all text and images are masked in replays, so they do not show your meals, photos, numbers or other on-screen content.

Diagnostic data is linked to your EatME account ID and may include your IP address. In addition, whenever the app communicates with our servers or directly with one of our service providers (for example, to sign you in or to upload a photo), standard technical information, such as your IP address, the time of the request and basic device and app details, is processed to handle the request.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your precise location, your contacts, audio recordings, advertising identifiers or payment details, and we do not read data from other health or fitness apps. We do not use your photos to identify people.

4. How AI processing works

EatME uses artificial intelligence ("AI") in two places. We access OpenAI's models through OpenRouter, a service that routes requests to AI model providers.

Creating your plan

When you finish the onboarding questions, our servers send your answers to an AI model, together with instructions for calculating daily nutrition targets. The model receives your gender, age, height, weight, goal, target weight, weekly pace, activity level and diet. It receives your age, not your date of birth. If the AI service is unavailable, we calculate your plan with a standard formula (the Mifflin-St Jeor equation) instead.

Analyzing your meals

When you scan a meal, the app resizes the photo and uploads it to our image storage provider, ImageKit. Our servers then send a smaller copy of the photo (at most 768 pixels on its longest side) to an AI vision model, which returns a name for the meal and estimates of its calories, protein, carbohydrates and fat. This analysis runs as a background job on Trigger.dev. If the model decides that the photo does not show food or a drink, the photo and the unfinished meal entry are deleted automatically.

What the AI providers do with your data

OpenRouter and OpenAI process the content we send only to return results to us. We do not permit them to use your content to train or improve their models, and where settings are available to us, we choose the ones that keep your content for the shortest time. Under their own terms, they may keep content for a limited period, for example to detect abuse.

Please do not include people, faces, documents, screens or other personal information in your meal photos.

AI results can be wrong

AI estimates can be inaccurate. Portion sizes, hidden ingredients such as oil or sugar, and the angle or lighting of a photo can all affect the result. Always review the estimates and correct them when needed. See Section 15.

9. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purposes described in this policy:

  • Account, profile and plan: for as long as you have an account. They are deleted when you delete your account.
  • Meals and nutrition values: until you delete the meal or your account.
  • Meal photos: until you delete the meal or your account. Photos that do not show food are deleted automatically right after analysis.
  • Onboarding answers before sign-up: stored on your device until you create an account. If you never create one, they are not saved in our database.
  • Background job records: Trigger.dev keeps the inputs, results and logs of jobs for a limited period set by our plan, after which they are deleted automatically.
  • Diagnostic data: Sentry keeps crash reports, logs, traces, replays and feedback for a limited period, typically no longer than 90 days, after which they are deleted automatically.
  • Messages you send us: for as long as we need to handle your request, and for a reasonable period afterwards in case you follow up.

After deletion, residual copies may remain for a short time in backups and in our providers' systems (for example, our database provider's restore history or cached copies of photos on the image delivery network) until they expire or are overwritten, normally within 30 days. We may keep information for longer where the law requires it or where it is needed to resolve a dispute or enforce our agreements.

10. Security

We use technical and organizational measures designed to protect your information, including:

  • encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) for connections between the app, our servers and our providers;
  • storage with established providers that encrypt the data they store for us;
  • sign-in checks on every request, so that your profile and meals are available only to your account;
  • keeping secret keys on our servers, never in the app, and limiting who can access production systems;
  • masking all text and images in session replays in the production app.

Meal photos are delivered through ImageKit's image delivery network using unique, unlisted links. We do not publish these links, but anyone who obtained a link could open the photo. This is another reason not to include people or personal documents in your meal photos.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a security incident affects your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities where the law requires it.

13. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: open Profile, tap Delete account and confirm. This permanently deletes:

  • your EatME account with our sign-in provider, Clerk;
  • your profile, onboarding answers and daily plan;
  • every meal you have logged; and
  • every meal photo stored with ImageKit.

Deletion starts immediately and cannot be undone. Residual copies may remain for a short time in backups and provider systems, as described in Section 9. Deleting the app from your phone does not delete your account.

If you cannot access the app, email [Contact Email] and we will delete your account after verifying that it is yours. Disconnecting EatME in your Apple or Google account settings stops future sign-ins but does not, by itself, delete the data we hold, so please use Delete account for that.

14. Children

EatME is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The app asks for your date of birth during onboarding and is designed to accept only users who are 13 or older.

If you are under 18, or under the age of majority where you live, you may use EatME only with the permission of a parent or legal guardian. In some countries, including in the EEA, a parent or guardian must also consent to the processing of your information if you are below the local age of digital consent (which can be as high as 16). Calorie tracking is not suitable for every young person, and we encourage parents to discuss it with their child and a health professional.

If you believe that a child under 13, or below the age of digital consent in their country, has given us personal information without the required consent, contact us at [Contact Email] and we will delete it.

15. Health information disclaimer

EatME provides estimates for general information and wellness purposes. It is not a medical device, and it does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

The calorie and nutrient values in the app are estimated by AI from photos and may be inaccurate or incomplete. Your plan is a general target based on the information you provide and does not take your medical history into account.

Talk to a doctor or registered dietitian before making significant changes to your diet, especially if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, are under 18, have a medical condition (such as diabetes, heart disease or kidney disease), take medication, or have or have had an eating disorder. If tracking food makes you feel anxious or leads to unhealthy habits, stop using the app and reach out to a health professional.
