A meal logged in three steps

Snap it, check it and see where your day stands. No weighing every ingredient.

  1. 1

    Snap it

    Take a photo of your plate, describe it in a few words or scan a barcode. Add a second photo or a note like “cooked in butter” for a closer estimate.

  2. 2

    Check the estimate

    AI names the foods and portions, and the numbers come from the USDA food database wherever a food matches. Wrong portion? Change it in a tap.

  3. 3

    See what’s left

    Calories, protein, carbs, fat, fiber and water fill up against your plan, so you know what’s left for the rest of the day.

An estimate you can check.

A number only helps if you can check it. Weight Class shows the foods and portions it saw, takes the numbers from the USDA food database wherever a food matches, and lets you change anything in a tap. It remembers your fixes for next time, and every calculation links to its sources in the app.

Tracking that fits into real life

Weight Class takes the busywork out of counting calories, so logging a meal takes seconds, not minutes.

  • A plan made for you

    Answer a few questions about your body, activity and goal, and get daily targets for calories, protein, carbs, fat, fiber and water. See how they’re worked out.

  • See the trend, not the noise

    A smoothed weight trend shows where you’re really heading, with your goal line, milestones, body measurements and private progress photos.

  • Plan tomorrow Premium

    In the evening, tap Draft tomorrow and Weight Class plans your day from meals you already eat, sized to your target. Swap or shuffle anything; nothing is logged until you tap Log it.

  • Calm mode

    When counting feels like too much, calm mode swaps calorie numbers for simple words, and you still see how your day is going.

  • Works with Apple Health

    Save your calories, macros, fiber and water to Apple Health, and see your steps, workouts and sleep next to your meals. That activity data stays on your phone.

  • Your data, your call

    Photos and descriptions go to AI only after you allow it, and the app names who gets them. No ads, never sold. Delete any meal, or your whole account, at any time.

Built for GLP-1 medicines

For people taking a GLP-1 medicine, with protein, fiber and water first while your appetite is lower.

  • Doses and sites. Log each dose and see where you injected last.
  • Pens and vials. Keep count of what’s left and when it’s due to be used.
  • Protein, fiber and water first. Targets that suit a smaller appetite.
  • Never dosing advice. Your prescriber decides; Weight Class keeps the log.

Questions, answered

Is Weight Class free?

Yes. Barcodes, food search, drinks, your goals, water, weight, body measurements, GLP-1 mode, supplements and 3 AI meal scans a day are free, with no ads. Weight Class Premium is optional: it adds unlimited AI scans, several photos of one meal, the weekly check-in and Plan tomorrow. You'll see the price in the app before you pay, and you're never charged without agreeing first.

How do I cancel Premium?

In Weight Class, Profile → Weight Class Premium → Manage subscription, or Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Premium stays on until the end of the period you paid for. More in Support.

Is Weight Class medical advice?

No. The calorie and nutrient numbers in Weight Class are estimates meant to help you stay aware of what you eat, and they can be wrong. Weight Class is not a medical device and doesn't replace a doctor or registered dietitian. Here's how Weight Class works out your numbers.

If you have a medical condition, take medication such as a GLP-1, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or have a history of disordered eating, please talk to a professional before changing how you eat.

Does Weight Class use AI, and where does my data go?

Only when you allow it. Before a photo or description goes to AI, Weight Class asks for your permission and names the companies involved, and you can turn it off at any time. Barcodes, food search, drinks and quick add never use AI. We never sell your data and show no ads. The Privacy Policy has the details.

Does Weight Class work with Apple Health?

Yes. Turn it on in Profile → Apple Health to save the calories, macros, fiber and water you log. You can also let Weight Class read your steps, workouts and sleep to show them next to your meals; that data stays on your phone.

How do I delete my account?

Open the app, go to Profile and tap Delete account. After you confirm, we permanently delete your account, your profile and plan, every meal you've logged and all of your photos.

Can't access the app? Use our account deletion page or email us at support@ugcmediakits.com. Our Privacy Policy explains the details.

Which devices does Weight Class work on?

iPhone, from the App Store. An Android version is on the way.
