FAQ

Questions, answered

Is EatME free?

Yes. EatME is free to use, with no ads. We may offer optional paid features in the future. If we do, you'll see the price in the app before you buy anything, and you'll never be charged without agreeing to it first.

Is EatME medical advice?

No. EatME's calorie and macro numbers are AI estimates meant to help you stay aware of what you eat, and they can be wrong. EatME is not a medical device and doesn't replace a doctor or registered dietitian.

If you have a medical condition, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or have a history of disordered eating, please talk to a professional before changing how you eat.

How do I delete my account?

Open the app, go to Profile and tap Delete account. After you confirm, we permanently delete your account, your profile and plan, every meal you've logged and all of your meal photos.
