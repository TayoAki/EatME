Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Last updated: [Effective Date]

This policy explains the consumer health data that [Company Legal Name] ("EatME", "we") collects through the EatME app, where it comes from, who it is shared with, and how you can access it, delete it or withdraw your consent, as required by Washington's My Health My Data Act.

1. Consumer health data we collect, and why

  • Body and goal details: your sex, date of birth, height, weight and weigh-ins, goal, goal weight, weekly pace, activity level and diet. We use them to calculate your daily calorie, macro, fiber and water targets and to show your progress.
  • Food and nutrition: the meals you log (photos, descriptions, notes, barcodes, foods and amounts), the calories, macros, vitamins and minerals estimated for them, and your water, saved meals, remembered foods and supplements. We use them to show what you ate against your targets.
  • Medicine (only if you turn on GLP-1 mode): the medicine, dose and schedule you enter, and the doses and side effects you log. We use them to show your dose day and your history. We never give dosing advice.
  • Estimates about you: what an AI model estimates a meal photo or description contains, and how confident it is. We use them to fill in your meals.
  • Your choices: whether you allowed AI analysis and turned on Apple Health or Health Connect sync. We use them to respect those choices.

We use consumer health data only to provide EatME to you. We don't sell it or use it for advertising.

2. Where it comes from

  • from you: what you enter and the photos you take in the app;
  • from EatME: the targets we calculate and the estimates made from what you give us; and
  • from public nutrition databases (USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts), for the nutrition facts of the foods and products you log, and, where the app offers restaurant menus, from fatsecret for the restaurant menu items you log.

3. Consumer health data we share, and with whom

We share consumer health data only with these service providers, which process it for us under contract:

  • Railway (hosting, database and photo storage): all of the data described above.
  • OpenRouter and OpenAI (AI analysis), only if you allow AI analysis: your onboarding answers, and the meal photos, descriptions and notes you choose to analyze, without your name or email address.
  • Sentry (crash reports), if crash reporting is on: limited technical details about an error, which can include the screen or action involved.

If you turn on sync, EatME writes your nutrition and water totals to Apple Health or Health Connect on your phone, at your direction. We have no affiliates, and we don't sell consumer health data.

4. Your rights and how to use them

  • Confirm and access: email [Contact Email] to ask whether we collect or share your consumer health data, for a copy of it, and for a list of the third parties we shared it with and how to contact them.
  • Delete: in the app (Profile → Delete account), on our account deletion page, or by email. We delete your data from our systems and our service providers', and from backups when they expire within 30 days.
  • Withdraw consent: turn off AI analysis in Profile → Preferences → AI meal analysis, turn off Apple Health or Health Connect sync in Profile, turn off or delete GLP-1 mode, or delete your account.

We answer requests within 45 days. If we need more time, we may extend this once by 45 days and will tell you why. We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours.

Appeal: if we don't act on your request, reply to our answer to appeal. We will tell you in writing what we decided within 45 days. If we deny your appeal, you can contact the Washington State Attorney General at atg.wa.gov/file-complaint.
